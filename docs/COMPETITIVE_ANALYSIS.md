## Implications for FAIRWAY IQ

The feature set is crowded. Scorecards, GPS, aggregate stats, personalized guidance and AI terminology are not sufficient standalone differentiation. In particular, SwingU's published practice/priority proposition overlaps our intended improvement journey. That overlap is evidence to sharpen testing, not evidence that our product cannot work.

Potential opportunities are **hypotheses**: less interpretation effort for 90–110 golfers; low-friction manual input for players who do not want detailed shot capture; a clearly bounded observation followed by one practical action; accessible progress reviews without false precision; reliable account-owned data and a consistent journey. None is shown to be absent from competitors or validated as a switching reason. No negative competitor UX, price-sensitivity or customer-satisfaction claim was established.

## Validation questions

1. How do golfers currently decide what to practice, including when their existing app already provides suggestions?
2. Which current tasks do they skip or misunderstand, and why? Ask for a recent example, not agreement with our hypothesis.
3. Is simpler input worth giving up shot-level analysis, or does it leave advice too generic?
4. Can they identify the evidence behind a summary and distinguish an observation from a diagnosis?
5. What concrete improvement would justify trying, continuing or paying for another app alongside an incumbent?
6. What data must be portable, and what account/privacy expectations would prevent switching?

Interview at least some current users of these categories, with no need to cover every brand in ten interviews. Follow [MARKET_VALIDATION_PLAN.md](MARKET_VALIDATION_PLAN.md). No interview, survey or willingness-to-pay result exists yet.
